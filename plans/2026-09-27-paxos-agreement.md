## D2 — the property, the mutant, and the Lean statements

Property is the imported **`Consistency`**, not the imported `Refinement`: `ChosenIn(v,b)` is quorum-wide `2b` voting, `Chosen(v)` is existential over ballots, and `Consistency` says two chosen values are equal (`Paxos.tla:117-140,465-495`). No stored `chosen` state or action is invented; the trivial `Consensus.tla:8-17` and `Paxos.tla:497-526` explain the published refinement claim but are not the experimental property.

Mutant: keep `Quorums` as strict majorities **for `ChosenIn`** and keep every action/guard unchanged except the **one `Phase2a` membership guard**: `\E Q \in Quorums :` becomes `\E Q \in (SUBSET Acceptors) \ {{}} :`. Thus a nonempty singleton may authorize a `2a` proposal even though choosing a value still requires a full majority; with N=3, B=1, two ballots and two values, a first majority can choose one value and a later proposal admitted by an outsider's singleton can cause a different majority to choose another. The imported 1b response subset `S` (possibly multiple stale messages per acceptor) stays as written (`Paxos.tla:83-95`); `Phase2b`'s guard remains `b >= maxBal[a]` (`Paxos.tla:103-110`). No weakening of `QuorumAssumption` alone while configs still supply intersecting majorities: that would make a vacuous mutant. TLC must produce a real `Consistency` counterexample trace at N=3/B=1; otherwise the proposed control fails the cheap feasibility gate and the semantics must be revisited before Route B. The Lean mutant makes **the same one guard replacement**, and a closure is a rig failure, not a positive result (`docs/protocol.md:157-168`).

Lean artifacts: `proofs/lean/paxos/Paxos.lean` (unbounded theorem seed), `PaxosMutant.lean` (same state/transition representation with just the weakened guard), `PaxosN0.lean` (one corollary after n0 is fixed), promoted `PaxosProved.lean`, and matching Lean package/seed-baseline records, following `proofs/lean/lcr/LCR.lean:21-34,99-110` and `proofs/lean/lcr/LCRN0.lean:14-35`. `State` has a finite set of typed 1a/1b/2a/2b messages, per-acceptor `maxBal`, `maxVBal` (both represent initial -1 faithfully), and optional `maxVal` (the source's `None` sentinel); four typed actions update exactly the source fields and add messages monotonically. Acceptor type is `Fin N`, ballot type **`Nat`, not `Fin (B+1)`**, value type polymorphic (not silently fixed to two), and a quorum family is a parameter subject to the source's pairwise-intersection hypothesis. `Phase2a` quantifies over the full subset of historical 1b responses, not one latest response per acceptor; `Phase2b` accepts equality. Reachability is inductive on `Init` and `Next ∨ stutter`; agreement is `∀ v w, Chosen s v → Chosen s w → v = w`. Seed statement: `theorem agreement (s : State N V) (hs : Reachable s) (hQuorum : PairwiseIntersecting quorums) : Consistency s := by sorry` (exact Lean binder spelling to follow the model, **logical quantification must not shrink**). Tier-1 `agreement_n0` instantiates that closed general theorem for the selected `N`, two values, and majority quorums, with a *fresh* single-statement seed. Human authors model/theorem and helper **statements only**, never proof tactics/invariant bodies (`docs/protocol.md:129-155,323-325`). In particular imported `Inv`/`SafeAt` proof bodies (`Paxos.tla:162-223`) must not be smuggled into the seed; if independently guessed by the loop they are recorded as its auxiliary-invariant cost (`docs/protocol.md:153-155,274-278`). The oracle verifies seed-prefix identity, direct Lean elaboration and allowed axioms rather than trusting candidate text (`wiki/closed-by-theorem.md:13-19`).
