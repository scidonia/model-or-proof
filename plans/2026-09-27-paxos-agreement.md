## D3 — how the crossing is located and `n0` is fixed

State the two adjacent rows, witnesses, both pilot totals, selected factor and ranges; if several qualifying brackets exist, choose the first by increasing N and record the others rather than tuning the choice after viewing final runs. Startup and the median cost distribution also appear separately. This definition is precommitted, not fitted to make the result look good.
